# Lesson 5.6 · Clouds of dots (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/core/src/match/points.ts` (`matchPoints`), and `scene.points()` in `packages/canvas/src/scene.ts`.
**Goal:** Albert can explain keyless point matching and when the greedy fallback kicks in.
**Mode:** fast, as in 5.1.

## A · Story
A heart made of cubes becomes a star. Each cube should travel to a *nearby* spot, not fly across. That's the Hungarian algorithm again (3.3), on distances.
- Predict the test "minimising total travel" (401 vs 181).
- Above 250,000 cells the cost table is too big, so pairing falls back to greedy closest-first.
- Leftovers shrink away or grow in. See the "Rebuilt from cubes" section in the playground.
