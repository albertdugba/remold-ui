# Lesson 2.3 · Speaking Apple (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/core/src/motion/spring.ts` (`springCoefficients`, `springs`) and the "Springs" section of `apps/playground`.
**Goal:** Albert can turn `{ duration, bounce }` into stiffness and damping and say what each preset feels like.
**Mode:** fast, as in 2.1.

## A · Story
Nobody knows what "stiffness 158, damping 25" feels like. "Half a second, a little bounce" everyone understands. Apple (WWDC23) found a formula between the two.

## B · Read
- `stiffness = (2π / duration)²`. Shorter duration means a stiffer band.
- `bounce = 0` gives `damping = 2√stiffness`: **critical damping**, the fastest way to arrive without overshooting. That's the first test.
- `bounce = 0.3` means a damping ratio of 0.7, which overshoots by about 4.6% (the test comment explains this).
- Ask: why does `bounce = 1` throw? *(Zero damping: it would bounce forever.)*

## C · Play
In the playground's "Springs" section, flip between Smooth, Snappy and Bouncy and watch the curve, then drag Bounce below 0. Ask him to describe each one in his own words.
