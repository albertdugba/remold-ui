# Lesson 5.5 · Looks (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/canvas/src/looks.ts`.
**Goal:** Albert can add a look.
**Mode:** fast, as in 5.1.

## A · Read
- `plate`, `chip`, `cylinder`, `tower` are all `prism()`: sides from the silhouette, a gradient across them, then the top face, then a label lying on the top.
- `glass`, `wire` and `grid` are flat.
- 🐛 Real bug found in Chrome: after dragging the scene round, labels read upside down. The fix flips text when the plane's x-axis points left (`plane[0] < 0`). Ask why that test works. *(It's the on-screen direction of the world's x-axis.)*
## B · Exercise
Write a `hexagon` look: an outline of 6 points, drawn with `prism`.
