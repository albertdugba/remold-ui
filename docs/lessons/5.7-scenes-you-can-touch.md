# Lesson 5.7 · Scenes you can touch (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/canvas/src/scene.ts` (`scene`) and `scene.test.ts`.
**Goal:** Albert can explain how layouts, focus and dragging reuse Part 2's springs and gestures.
**Mode:** fast, as in 5.1.

## A · Read
- Every number is a `value()` channel, so `to(layout)` is interruptible and keeps momentum (test: "going back mid-flight keeps the momentum").
- The yaw turns the short way round (`applyCamera`) even after the scene has been spun by hand.
- Drag: `hold()` the yaw, `set()` it under the finger, then `release({ velocity, target: current + project(v) })`. That's 2.4 and 2.5 together.
- Accessibility: `role="application"`, arrow keys move focus in reading order, a live description, and reduced motion jumps instead of moving.
- 🐛 Real bug found in Chrome: `setPointerCapture` throws when the pointer is already gone, which silently broke tap-to-focus. Now wrapped in a try/catch. Ask why capture is optional.
