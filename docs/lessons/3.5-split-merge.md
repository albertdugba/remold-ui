# Lesson 3.5 · Splitting and merging (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/core/src/match/split-merge.ts` (`splitsAndMerges`).
**Goal:** Albert can explain when a set of parts counts as a whole.
**Mode:** fast, as in 3.1.

## A · Story
"12 Rue de Rivoli, 75001 Paris" became two lines. Neither line alone is the address, but together they are. And "Ada" + "Lovelace" became "Ada Lovelace".

## B · Read
- `isPartOf`: every significant word of the part is in the whole, and the whole has more.
- Each part claims its closest whole; a whole needs at least 2 parts, covering at least 60% of its words.
- Pairs that get absorbed are removed from the one-to-one list.
- Ask: why the 60% rule? *(Two short labels that happen to share words with a paragraph aren't a split of it.)*

The Checkout scenario in the playground: the address, the name and the item list.
