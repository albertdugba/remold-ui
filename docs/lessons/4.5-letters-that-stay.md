# Lesson 4.5 · Letters that stay (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/dom/src/text-morph.ts` (`morphText`, `sharedLetters`, `graphemes`).
**Goal:** Albert can explain the longest common subsequence, and why runs shorter than two letters are dropped.
**Mode:** fast, as in 4.1.

## A · Story
"Saving changes" → "Saved": "Sav" stays put, "ed" arrives, "ing changes" leaves.

## B · Read
- `sharedLetters`: an LCS table, then keep only runs of 2+. Predict `sharedLetters("cat", "dog")`. *(Nothing.)* Why drop lone letters? *(A single shared "e" flying across a word looks like a glitch.)*
- `graphemes` uses `Intl.Segmenter`, so 👍🏽 stays one letter.
- Accessibility: the real text sits in a visually hidden span; the letters are `aria-hidden`.
- Interruptible: a new call starts from where the letters are, with their speed.
