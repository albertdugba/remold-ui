# Lessons 5.2 and 5.3 · Moving the paper, and the 3D-ish camera (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/canvas/src/camera.ts` (`projector`, `iso`, `flat`, `fitCamera`) and `geometry.test.ts`.
**Goal:** Albert can project a 3D point by hand and explain `plane(z)`.
**Mode:** fast, as in 5.1.

## A · Story
Put a toy on a table, turn the table (**yaw**), then lean back so you see it at an angle (**pitch**). Pitch 0 is looking straight down, which gives a flat diagram.
## B · Read `project`
- Turn: `x' = cos·x − sin·y`, `y' = sin·x + cos·y`. Tilt: squash y' by cos(pitch), and lift by z·sin(pitch).
- Predict, for a flat camera on an 800px canvas: where does (100, 50) land? *(500, 482. The origin sits at 54% of the height, which is the first projector test.)*
- `plane(z)` is the same mapping as a `setTransform` matrix, so text and grids can be drawn lying on a surface. The test "plane(z) is the same mapping as project" proves they agree.
- `fitCamera` makes the "Diagram" layouts fit any canvas.
