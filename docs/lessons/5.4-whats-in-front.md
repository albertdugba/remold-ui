# Lesson 5.4 · What's in front (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/canvas/src/geometry.ts` (`silhouette`, `convexHull`, `pointInPolygon`, `paintOrder`).
**Goal:** Albert can explain why drawing and hit testing use the same silhouette.
**Mode:** fast, as in 5.1.

## A · Painting order
Painters paint the far mountains first. `paintOrder`: lower things first (so what sits on a shelf comes after the shelf), then farther before nearer.
## B · Silhouettes
An extruded convex shape seen from any angle is the hull of its top and bottom outlines. Sides are filled from it, and `pick` tests the pointer against it. Ask: what bug does sharing it prevent? *(A click that misses something you can clearly see.)*
- Test "covers the sides of a tall shape": pointing halfway up a tower hits it.
