# Lesson 3.6 · Keeping score (script for Claude)

**Files:** `packages/core/src/match/benchmark/` (`build.ts`, `scenarios.ts`, `score.ts`, `benchmark.test.ts`) and the "What became what" section of `apps/playground`.
**Goal:** Albert can add a scenario, read precision and recall, and explain why the matcher has to beat both baselines.
**Mode:** fast, as in 3.1.

## A · Story
"It feels better" isn't evidence. Hand-label the right answers, count how many the matcher gets right (**precision**: of what it claimed, how much was right) and how much of the truth it found (**recall**). F1 combines them.

## B · Baselines
- **Tree position**: what React does without keys. It's terrible when markup is rebuilt (Dashboard: 3.6%).
- **Exact text**: it misses every reworded or updated value.
- The current results (hand-built scenarios): Remold 99.0%, exact text 71.2%, tree position 32.5%.

## C · Honesty
These scenarios were written knowing how the matcher works, so they flatter it. The prototype's 95.3% was on **held-out** pages. Ask him: how would you make a fairer test? *(Have someone else write scenarios without seeing the code, or port `prototypes/engine-v0/pages-heldout.tsx`.)* That's a good first contribution.
