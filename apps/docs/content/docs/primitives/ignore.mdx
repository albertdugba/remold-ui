title: "Ignore"
description: "Leaves its contents out of transitions."
---

## Installation

<Install packages="@remold-ui/react" vanilla="@remold-ui/dom" />

## Usage

<CodeVariants>
<Variant framework="react">

```tsx
<Remold.Layout trigger={view}>
  <Remold.Ignore>
    <LiveChart />
  </Remold.Ignore>
</Remold.Layout>
```

</Variant>
<Variant framework="js">

```html
<!-- Anything inside data-rm-ignore is left out of matching and animation. -->
<section id="dashboard">
  <div data-rm-ignore>
    <canvas id="live-chart"></canvas>
  </div>
</section>
```

</Variant>
</CodeVariants>

Anything inside is left out of transitions: it isn't measured, paired or moved. Use it for videos, live charts and anything that animates by itself. It renders a `span` with `data-rm-ignore`; you can put that attribute on any element instead.
