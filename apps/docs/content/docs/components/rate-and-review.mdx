## Things to try

- **Pick a star.** Hover to preview, then tap: the stars fill one after another, then all five shrink and fly up into the header beside a word for your rating, as the card grows into the review.
- **Change your mind.** The small stars in the header still work, and the word changes with them.
- **Write a little.** Tap a few quick notes, add a sentence, and post. The button says it's posting, then the form folds into your review: your words quoted, your notes underneath, and Edit to open it again.
- **Not now** puts the card back to the big stars.

## How it works

- **One value.** `value` holds the `stage` (`rate`, `write`, `posting` or `posted`), the `rating`, the chosen `tags` and the `text`. It works controlled or uncontrolled. Posting is yours to finish: set the stage to `posted` when your server says so; the demo waits a moment.
- **The same five stars, in two places.** The big row and the header row are two renderings of the same stars, each star sharing a Motion `layoutId` across them. Rating hands them over, so each star flies to its new place, a few milliseconds behind the one before.
- **The card grows to fit.** Its height springs to whatever it holds, measured as it changes, the way the table booking card does. The form's parts arrive a little small and blurred and settle, one after another.
- **Words that change in place.** The line above the item's name goes from the delivery date to a word for your rating, and the button from "Post review" to "Posting", with `Remold.Text`.
- **A shop's colours.** Orange stars, a yellow Post button and teal links and focus rings, the way a big marketplace does it. They are six variables at the top of the file: `--rr-star`, `--rr-star-edge`, `--rr-action`, `--rr-action-hover`, `--rr-on-action` and `--rr-link`.
- **Accessible.** The stars are a Base UI `RadioGroup`, so arrow keys move between them and each says what it means. The quick notes are a `ToggleGroup` and the text box a `Field`. Focus moves into the text box when the form opens and to Edit once it's posted, and each step is announced.
