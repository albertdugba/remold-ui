## Things to try

- **Pick a new time.** The card springs into the updated booking, and the time you chose travels from its option into the booking's header while the notice and the other option step out of the way.
- **Change the party.** Press the guest count to open the party-size picker. Pick a number and you're back on the same booking with the new count already in place.
- **Go back** with the arrow. The booking settles back into the options it came from. Hover the confirmation code to see what it's for.

## How it works

- **One card, three faces.** The notice, the booking and the party-size picker are faces of one card. It springs its real height between them and clips what's inside, so nothing is scaled.
- **The time carries over.** The time you pick is the same chip in the option and in the booking, so Motion's `layoutId` moves it without resizing it. The grey card behind it travels too: an empty shape that springs from the option row into the ticket and back, handed over whole so it never fades or stretches.
- **Everything else arrives a beat later.** Words scale in from just below their size, unblurring. Filled boxes, like the warning, scale in without the blur, which would otherwise square off their corners.
- **Built on Base UI.** `Button` for the options and every press, `ToggleGroup` and `Toggle` for the party size, whose highlight slides to your pick, and `Tooltip` on the confirmation code.
