## Things to try

- **Drag the slider, or type a number.** Faces join and leave your team and the price rolls like a counter.
- **Cross a tier.** The plan steps up and brings its extra features with it.
- **Switch to yearly.** The monthly price is struck through and the saving is shown.
- **Go past the last tier.** The price gives way to a conversation.

## How it works

- **One number drives everything.** The slider and the number field are Base UI's Slider and Number Field bound to the same seat count, and every other part is derived from it.
- The faces, features and plan changes are Motion; the price rolls with a small odometer. Billing period is Base UI's Toggle Group.
