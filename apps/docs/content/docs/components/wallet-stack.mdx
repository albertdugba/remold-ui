## Things to try

- **Tap the holder.** The cards slide up out of the front pocket, fanned so you can read each one, still tucked in at the bottom. Tap again and they slip back in.
- **Choose a card.** It rises to the top while the holder sinks out of the way, and its latest transactions arrive row by row in the space between.
- **Make it your default.** Flip the switch and the Default badge moves over to it.

## How it works

- **Springs, a beat apart.** Every card and the holder are placed by Motion springs, staggered slightly, so each change reads as one movement rather than a jumble.
- **Drawn leather.** The holder is SVG: a pebbled grain from a noise filter, a burnished edge and close stitching. Nothing is an image.
- The default-card switch is Base UI's Switch.
