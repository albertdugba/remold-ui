It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Type an email and press Enter, Space or a comma.** It becomes a chip with the person's initials and their name, when the address has one in it (`maya.ortiz@…` reads as Maya Ortiz).
- **Paste several at once.** A list separated by commas, spaces or new lines becomes a chip each. Backspace in an empty field takes the last one back.
- **Press Invite.** Each chip moves down into the pending list, and they accept one at a time: each avatar travels from its row up into the stack, and the rows below close up.
- **Cancel one while it's pending.** It leaves the list and never joins.
- **Point at the stack.** It opens out so every face can be told apart. Past five people it counts the rest.
- **Try a bad address, or someone already invited.** The status line says what's wrong.

## How it works

- The chip, the pending row and the stack's avatar are three different elements in three different parts of the card. One [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) wraps the card, and it matches them by what they show: the name and the initials. So the person moves from one to the next instead of disappearing and reappearing.
- Layout runs when the people change: a chip added, an invite sent, someone joining, the stack opening. Typing in the field doesn't animate anything.
- The stack opening on hover is a layout change too: the avatars' overlap becomes a gap, and Layout moves them there.
- The invites are pretend. Send your real ones in `send` and call `join` when someone accepts.
