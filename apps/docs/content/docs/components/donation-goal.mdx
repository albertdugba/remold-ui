It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Give.** Pick an amount and press Donate. A stream of dots rises from below the drop and settles at the waterline, so the drop fills by exactly the share you added.
- **Watch the numbers.** The total and the backer count roll and the bar grows.
- **Reach the goal.** The water scatters and gathers into a heart, and the buttons give way to a thank-you line. The reset button empties the drop back to where it began.

## How it works

- **One set of dots, always the same size.** The drop is sampled once into points, ordered from the bottom up. Every frame draws all of them: a faint copy for the empty drop and a second copy for the water. `fit` is the picture's frame, so the drop stays still while it fills.
- **Filling is moving dots.** Water that isn't raised yet waits, invisible, below the drop. A gift moves the next few into their places in the drop, a handful at a time, so they arrive as a stream.
- **Colour is a tone.** Each dot carries a tone that springs with it, and the palette maps it to empty, water or hidden. The colours come from the card's CSS variables, so they follow dark mode.
- **The heart is another shape.** At the goal the particles flow into a heart path drawn in the same frame, with a scatter transition, then back into the drop when it starts again.

<Callout>The campaign and the backers are made up. Swap the drop for the shape of what your campaign pays for: any SVG path works.</Callout>
