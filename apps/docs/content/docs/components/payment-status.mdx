## Things to try

- **Watch it play.** The card floats, becomes a lock whose shackle clicks shut, comes back with a warning when the bank says no, then goes through and becomes a receipt.
- **Pick a status.** It stops playing and jumps there. Go from Card to Paid and the card reshapes straight into the receipt.

## How it works

- **Status in, drawing out.** Pass `status` as `card`, `processing`, `declined` or `paid`, straight from your payment's state, along with the `amount`, the `card` and the receipt `email`.
- **Layers that reshape.** The card, the lock and the receipt are the same `Remold.Icon` layers drawn differently, so each change morphs instead of swapping. The accent turns red for a refusal and green once it's paid.
- **Motion that says what's happening.** The shackle clicks while the bank confirms, and the card shakes its head when it's declined. Both are plain CSS and hold still while the drawing reshapes.
