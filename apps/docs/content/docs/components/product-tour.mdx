## Things to try

- **Press Take the tour.** The pill in the corner grows into a welcome card in the middle of the app, and everything dims. A step with nothing to point at sits in the middle, with no line.
- **Press Next.** The window closes in on New report and a dotted line runs from it to the tip, with a light travelling along it. From here the window springs from control to control, and the tip's title and text change letter by letter.
- **Watch each control answer its step.** Search types a query, the revenue change lights up, this week's point on the chart shows its number, Reports gets a badge and two teammates slide in beside Share.
- **Notice where the tip goes.** Below the small controls, above the chart, to the right of the sidebar and to the left of Share, and it stays inside the app whichever side it's on.
- **Use the arrow keys,** or Back and Next. Escape ends the tour from any step.
- **Finish.** The window opens past the edges, the dim fades, and the tip folds back into the pill it came from, ready for next time.
- **Customize it.** Change the accent, how much it dims, or the line's style and shape, or turn the line or its light off; the code below the controls updates to match.

## Make it yours

- **Your look** is props, set live in Customize above: `accent`, how far the app `dim`s, and the line, which you can turn off (`connector={false}`) or restyle with `connectorColor`, `connectorStyle`, `connectorShape` and `pulse`. Below a dim of 0.25 the ring and the line take the accent, so they still show on a bright app.
- **Your steps** go in `steps` (the default is `STEPS`): a `name`, a title, a line of text, and which `side` of its control the tip sits on. A step points at the `Remold.Tour.Target` with its name; leave the target out and the step sits in the middle, like the welcome and the goodbye.
- **Your reactions:** a target carries `data-rm-active` while its step shows. The badge, the tooltip and the teammates here are plain CSS on that attribute (`group-data-rm-active:` in Tailwind).
- **Your app** is the mock between the comments. Mark each control you want to point at with `Remold.Tour.Target` (rendered through a `Remold.Anchor` of the same `name`, for the line), and use that name as a step's `target`.

## How it works

- **The tour itself** is [Tour](/docs/primitives/tour): it holds the step, drives the arrow keys and Escape, and its Back, Next and close buttons move it.
- **The spotlight** is `Tour.Spotlight` with a wide shadow that dims everything outside it. It springs to each control with a CSS transition whose easing is a spring curve, so it overshoots a touch and settles.
- **The tip is the help button.** One element is both. When the tour starts, a [Morph](/docs/primitives/morph) grows it out of the corner into the tip. Each step, it moves the tip to its new place. At the end, it folds the tip back into the pill.
- **The line** is [Connectors](/docs/primitives/connectors): each control is an anchor, and the tip links to the current one. Changing the link draws the old line out and the new one in, and `pulse={step}` sends a light along it on every step. It stays attached while the tip moves.
- **The words** are [Text](/docs/primitives/text), so the title, the text and "1 of 4" change letter by letter instead of swapping.
- **Every control is measured ahead of time** with `react-use-measure`, and again when it or the window resizes, so the tip moves in the same render that changes the step.
- **It's a dialog while it's open.** It's labelled by the tip's title and described by its text. Focus moves to Next when the tour starts and back to the pill when it ends. The app behind is `inert`, so keyboard focus stays in the tip.
