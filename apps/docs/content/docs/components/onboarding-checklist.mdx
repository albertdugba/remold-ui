It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Do the open step.** The button says what's happening ("Connecting…"), the step's icon turns into a tick, the ring and the count move on, and the next step opens. The app behind it changes too: connect a source and it appears in the sidebar and the empty state turns into a connected card.
- **Invite your team, then build the dashboard.** Avatars slide into the header, and the dashboard's tiles arrive in the same motion as the checklist.
- **Skip for now.** The step moves to a Skipped group at the bottom and the line joining the steps re-routes around the gap. **Do it now** brings it back.
- **Minimise.** The checklist docks into a pill that shows the ring and the next step. Click it to open it again.
- **Finish every step.** The checklist collapses into a "You're all set" card with every tick in a row.

## How it works

- The open card, the docked pill and the done card are three different pieces of markup, and the app behind them changes as well. One [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) wraps all of it, so whatever they share (the title, the count, the ticks, the tiles) moves instead of being swapped out.
- Each step's mark is an [Anchor](/docs/primitives/connectors) that links to the step before it. [Connectors](/docs/primitives/connectors) with `end="top"` draws one straight line down through them, and redraws it as steps open, close and move.
- [Icon](/docs/primitives/icon) morphs the step's own Lucide icon into a tick. [Text](/docs/primitives/text) changes the count and the button label in place.
- The scene is a list of whole states played by [`useAutoplay`](/docs/hooks/use-autoplay), which stops as soon as someone touches it.
