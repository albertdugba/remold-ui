It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Tick a step.** The next one opens in place while the finished one settles back, and the ring fills as you go.
- **Finish the last step.** The whole card folds down into a small "All set" pill that stays out of your way.
- **Hide it early.** The pill remembers how far you got; open it to bring the card back.

## How it works

- **Card to pill.** The card and the pill are two different pieces of markup, so [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) animates the change: it pairs the ring, the title and the count across both shapes, with no ids.
- The ring and the ticks are Motion; the checkboxes are Base UI's Checkbox.
