## Things to try

- **Open the bell.** It grows into the inbox, the rows rising in one after another, and the bell turns into the close button.
- **Move over the list.** One soft highlight slides from row to row.
- **Open a notification** and its dot goes. The count on the bell rolls down to match.
- **Answer the invite** in place. Accept or Decline gives way to what you chose.
- **Mark all as read.** The dots clear top to bottom and the count ticks down with them. On Unread, the rows fold away until you're all caught up.

## How it works

- **One shell morphs,** like the [Popover menu](/docs/components/popover-menu)'s: a flat surface springs from the bell's circle to the inbox's size and back. Its height follows the content, measured, so it grows and shrinks as rows come and go.
- **The panel is Base UI's [Popover](https://base-ui.com/react/components/popover),** portalled into that shell. It handles focus, Escape and clicking outside.
- **The count is derived,** never stored: it's how many notices aren't read. The bell rings whenever it goes up, and the number rolls with an odometer whenever it changes.
- **Rows slide, never jump.** Day names and rows sit in one list; a row leaving fades out of the flow and the rest slide up on a spring, while the shell follows them.
- **Marking all as read** clears one notice every 70 ms, top to bottom, so the count ticks down instead of snapping to nothing.
- **Changes are spoken.** A new notification and "Mark all read" are announced through a polite live region with Remold's `announce`, so a screen reader hears the count change without focus moving. Reading a notification yourself isn't announced. The demo stays quiet while it plays by itself; pass `announcements={false}` to do the same.
- **Each row is one button** that opens it, sitting under the row's content, so the invite's own buttons still work inside it.
- **All or Unread** is a Base UI Toggle Group with a sliding highlight, like the hover.
