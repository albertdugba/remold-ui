## Things to try

- **Pick a card.** The one you tap grows into a full-size note, and the cursor is already in it.
- **Write a few words** on the ruled note. Stuck? Tap a starter like "Happy birthday" to fill in a first line; the starters step aside once you've written something.
- **Switch cards with the dots** under the note. It takes the new colour and keeps your words, and the main button follows.
- **Add the note.** It slips down into an envelope, the flap folds shut and a seal in the card's colour presses onto it. A moment later the envelope tucks into the order, with your message shown as a small note underneath.
- **Edit or remove it** from that line.

## How it works

- **One value.** `value` holds the `stage` (`pick`, `write`, `sealing` or `added`), the chosen `design` and the `message`. It works controlled or uncontrolled; sealing finishes on its own and reports `reason: "timer"`. Pass the `designs`, and who it's `to` and `from`.
- **One note, three sizes.** The small card, the note you write on and the note in the envelope share a Motion `layoutId`, so the card you picked is the one you write on and the one that's sealed. The envelope shares one with the icon in the order line.
- **A flap that folds.** The envelope's flap is a triangle turned back on its hinge that springs shut once the note is in, then the seal pops on.
- **A gentle theme.** The main button starts a soft pink and takes on a deeper tone of the card you pick; each design passes its own `accent`.
- **Springy, like the rest.** Parts arrive a little small and blurred and settle on the shared spring; the card's height springs to whatever it holds.
- **Accessible.** The designs are buttons named after them, the message is a Base UI `Field` labelled with who it's to, focus moves into it when it opens, and adding the note is announced.
