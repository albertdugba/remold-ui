## Things to try

- **Tap in an amount.** Each digit rolls in from below and the commas make room as the number grows. A long amount shrinks to fit. On a computer you can also click the amount and type.
- **Go over your balance.** The digit that would take you past it is refused with a shake, rather than accepted and flagged later.
- **Send.** The keypad gives way to Kwame's avatar with a hairline ring drawing round it while the arrow becomes a spinner. When the money lands, the ring closes in green, his avatar gives a small bump, a tick pops onto its corner, and the button says Done.

## How it works

- **Arrow, spinner, check.** The Send button's icon is one `Remold.Icon` reshaped from icon to icon, so each state grows out of the last.
- **The ring is real progress.** It's an SVG circle whose `pathLength` eases most of the way round while the transfer runs and springs closed when it lands, so it never claims to be done before it is.
- The rolling digits, the shake, the fit-to-width and the tick that pops in are Motion.
