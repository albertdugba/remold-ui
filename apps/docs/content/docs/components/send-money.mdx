## Things to try

- **Tap in an amount.** Each digit rolls in from below and the commas make room as the number grows. A long amount shrinks to fit. On a computer you can also click the amount and type.
- **Go over your balance.** The digit that would take you past it is refused with a shake, rather than accepted and flagged later.
- **Send.** The arrow turns into a spinner, then a check.

## How it works

- **Arrow, spinner, check.** The Send button's icon is one path reshaped with Remold's `createMorph`, so each state grows out of the last.
- The rolling digits, the shake and the fit-to-width are Motion.
