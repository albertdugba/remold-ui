## How it works

The two fields never move; only what they say changes. Without Remold the codes would just flip. [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) sees that "LHR" is now in the other field, matching it by its text, and moves it there, while JFK makes the opposite trip.

`RouteField` is exported on its own, so a bigger surface can put it inside its own Layout. The [Flight booking](/docs/components/flight-booking) does exactly that.
