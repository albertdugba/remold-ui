## Things to try

- **Press "Try sample files".** The empty state folds into a slim "Add more files" strip, its icon and words moving to their new places, and five files drop into the list. The spreadsheet among them is refused straight away.
- **Watch a row upload.** The row itself fills from the left while its size line counts up ("35% · 4.9 MB of 14.2 MB"). When it's done the fill fades, the size settles back in, and a small green tick pops in; hover the row and the tick turns back into the remove button.
- **Watch the big PDF get turned away.** At 32.4 MB it's over the 25 MB limit, so its row shakes, turns red and offers Compress. Press it: the file shrinks to 14.2 MB and uploads.
- **Drag files over the card.** A dashed drop area appears, the icon lifts and turns into an arrow, and the label counts what you're holding: "Let go to upload 3 files".
- **Drop, paste or choose your own.** Only images and PDFs go in. Images show a thumbnail and PDFs get a red tile. Anything else, or anything over 25 MB, is refused with the reason.
- **Drag something else over it.** Drag a spreadsheet or a video over the drop zone and it turns red and says it only takes images and PDFs, before you let go.
- **Remove a row.** It fades and the rows below slide up. While a file is uploading, the same button cancels it.
- **Read the top line.** It re-letters from "No files yet" to "Uploading 3 files", with how much is left beside it, to "4 files uploaded", or says how many failed.

Nothing is sent anywhere: the uploads are simulated.

## How it works

- **You own the files.** The component shows a list of items with a status and a progress from 0 to 1, and tells you what was added, removed or retried. Wire those to your own upload code.
- **The fold is a Remold morph.** The empty state and the strip are two layouts of the same button. `Remold.Morph` works out what became what, so the icon and words travel to their new places while the rest fades.
- **The card follows its content.** The content is measured and the card's height springs to it, so rows arriving and leaving never make it jump.
- **The row is the progress.** Base UI's `Progress` sits behind each row and fills it from the left, so there's no separate bar to push the text around. When the file is done the fill fades and a small green tick pops in; it gives way to the remove button on hover or focus.
- **Rows make room.** Each row animates its position, so removing one slides the rest up while it fades.
- **It works without a mouse.** The zone is a button that opens the file picker, pasting files works anywhere in the card, and removing a row moves focus to the next one.
