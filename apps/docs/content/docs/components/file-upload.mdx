## Things to try

- **Press "Add sample files".** The drop zone folds into a strip and five files drop into the list. The spreadsheet among them is refused straight away.
- **Watch a row finish.** Its bar pulls away to the right and a green check pops in where the percentage was.
- **Watch the big PDF fail.** It stops at 62%, the row shakes and turns red, and a Retry button appears. Press it and it goes through.
- **Drag files over the card.** The zone darkens, the icon lifts, and the label counts what you're holding: "Let go to upload 3 files".
- **Drop, paste or choose your own.** Only images and PDFs go in. Images show a thumbnail and PDFs get a red tile. Anything else, or anything over 25 MB, is refused with the reason.
- **Drag something else over it.** Drag a spreadsheet or a video over the drop zone and it turns red and says it only takes images and PDFs, before you let go.
- **Remove a row.** It fades and the rows below slide up. While a file is uploading, the same button cancels it.
- **Read the top line.** It re-letters from "Uploading 2 of 4" to "All 4 uploaded", or says how many failed.

Nothing is sent anywhere: the uploads are simulated.

## How it works

- **You own the files.** The component shows a list of items with a status and a progress from 0 to 1, and tells you what was added, removed or retried. Wire those to your own upload code.
- **The card follows its content.** The content is measured and the card's height springs to it, so rows arriving and leaving never make it jump.
- **The bar becomes the check.** When a row finishes, the bar shrinks toward the right edge while the check springs in beside it, so it reads as one thing turning into the other.
- **Rows make room.** Each row animates its position, so removing one slides the rest up while it fades.
- **It works without a mouse.** The zone is a button that opens the file picker, pasting files works anywhere in the card, and removing a row moves focus to the next one.
