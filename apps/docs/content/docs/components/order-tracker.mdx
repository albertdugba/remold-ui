## Things to try

- **Change how many.** The count and the price roll to their new values, and the button's total follows.
- **Order it.** The button says it's placing the order. Then the rest of the card blurs away while the red button bends into the pot's body, its red deepening to ink, and the lid, steam and stripe settle in around it.
- **Watch it arrive.** The drawing reshapes from the pot to the bag, the scooter and the front door, and the line under it says what's happening. Pick any step from the bars to jump there.
- **Order again.** Once it's delivered, the dark Order again pill grows back into the card, its ink softening to the card's white, and the dish and details settle in after it.

## How it works

- **The button becomes the pot.** The button is redrawn as a pill-shaped path exactly where it sits, and `Remold.Icon` reshapes it into the pot's body from the tracker's first drawing, so the drawing that takes over is already in place. Everything else about the card fades with Motion.
- **The order is one drawing.** The pot, the bag, the scooter and the door are one set of `Remold.Icon` layers that reshape from stage to stage, and each stage plays its own little loop: the lid rattles, the bag hops, the wheels spin, the door swings.
- **Numbers that roll.** The quantity, the price and the button's total are `Remold.Text`.
- The dish photo comes from `PHOTO` at the top of the file. Point it at your own image, and set `--fo-brand` for your brand colour.

<Callout>The restaurant and the prices are made up. The install command doesn't copy the photo: add your own image to your `public` folder and set `PHOTO` to its path.</Callout>
