## Things to try

- **Press Delete account.** The button's red tint grows into the dialog and turns white as it arrives, then the list of what you lose fades in.
- **Type the email.** The grey characters fill in as you match them, the count under the field re-letters, and the button fills from the left.
- **Type a wrong character.** The field shakes and turns red, and the button stops filling until you fix it.
- **Finish the email.** The button pops as it unlocks. Press it, or press Enter.
- **Delete.** The dialog shrinks into a dark bar at the bottom with Undo and a ring that counts down six seconds.
- **Undo.** Your account comes back. Wait instead, and it's gone for good.
- **Cancel, or press Escape.** The dialog shrinks back into the button.

## How it works

- **One surface, three places.** The button's tint, the dialog's sheet and the undo bar share a Motion layout id, so it's one surface that grows, then shrinks into the bar. It's an empty shape, so no words stretch; the content fades in once it has arrived.
- **Characters fill in.** The email sits in grey behind the field in the same type, so what you type lands exactly on top of it. Any text is accepted, including paste and dictation; it only counts once it matches.
- **The button fills with a clip.** The red layer carries its own white label and is clipped to how much of the email you've matched, so the label reads on both sides of the edge.
- **Focus follows the action.** Opening puts focus in the field. Deleting moves it to Undo, since the button that opened the dialog no longer exists.
