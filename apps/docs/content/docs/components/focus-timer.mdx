It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Start and pause.** Sand streams through the neck and piles up below while the clock counts down. Pause and the stream hangs in the air until you resume.
- **Skip.** The rest of the sand rushes down, the hourglass flips, and the break starts full. Skipping a break moves you on to the next round.
- **Reset.** The sand pours back up into the top bulb and the clock returns to the start of the session.
- **Watch the rounds.** The pill in the corner moves along as you finish each focus and break, and turns green while you rest.

## How it works

- **The sand is a fixed set of grains.** The top bulb is filled with a grid of grains, ordered from the surface down. The bottom bulb holds the same grains mirrored, so grain 10 always has a place in each bulb.
- **Time is how many have fallen.** The share of the session that has passed decides how many grains sit in the bottom pile. The next few wait in a thin stream between the neck and the pile, so as time moves on each grain slides down the stream and lands.
- **Flipping is a rotation, not a rebuild.** At the end of a session the canvas turns half a circle, and the grains are laid out mirrored from then on. A full bottom bulb is already a full top bulb, so nothing jumps.
- **Colour is a tone.** Glass and sand each carry a tone, and the palette comes from the card's CSS variables, so it follows dark mode.

<Callout>The demo runs fast so you can watch a whole session. Set `SPEED` to `{ focus: 1, break: 1 }` for real time, and change `FOCUS`, `BREAK` and `ROUNDS` to suit your app.</Callout>
