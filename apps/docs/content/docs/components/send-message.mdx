It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Start typing.** The mic turns into a send arrow.
- **Send.** Your message rises out of the composer into the thread, pushing the others up.
- **Wait for a reply.** Their face slides down to your message once they've read it, a typing bubble bounces in, and their reply takes its place.

## How it works

- **Mic to arrow.** The composer's button reshapes between the two icons with Remold's `createMorph`.
- **The same face, a newer message.** The read receipt is one small avatar moving to the latest message it has seen, which is what Motion's `layoutId` is for.

<Callout>The people are made up. Avatars are illustrations from [DiceBear](https://www.dicebear.com).</Callout>
