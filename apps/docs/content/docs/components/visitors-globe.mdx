The visitors arrive on their own. Touch the globe and the turning is yours.

## Things to try

- **Watch it live.** Every couple of seconds something happens. Signups pulse green and upgrades pulse amber at their city, invites fly a violet arc from one city to another, the latest one floats up as a toast, and the count rolls.
- **Spin it.** Drag the planet. Let go mid-flick and it coasts, then settles back into its slow turn.

## How it works

- **The planet is one component.** [Remold.Globe](/docs/primitives/globe) draws the land, the ocean and the glow, turns it, and handles dragging. The card passes it the recent signups and upgrades as `pulses`, the invites as `arcs`, and the busiest cities as `markers`.
- **Each event fires once.** Every event has an id, and the globe only acts on ids it hasn't seen, so the toast and the planet stay in step without extra wiring.
- **A horizon, not a globe.** The canvas is bigger than the card and sits low, so only the top of the planet shows and the card clips the rest. A soft fade at the bottom keeps the toast and legend readable.
- **Events land in view.** Cities take turns in the order they come round as it spins, and invites go to a city that's already passed, so arcs sweep across the face you can see.

<Callout>The visitors and the cities are made up. Feed the list from your analytics events, and add the cities you see most.</Callout>
