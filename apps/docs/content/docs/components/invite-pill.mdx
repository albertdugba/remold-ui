## Things to try

- **Press Invite.** The plus turns into a mail icon, the faces tuck closer together, and the pill opens into an email field.
- **Type an email and press Enter.** Send says Sent, the field folds away, and the new face joins the front of the stack in green before it settles in.
- **Try a bad address, or someone already there.** The button says what's wrong. Typing again clears it.
- **Press Escape, or click away.** The pill closes without sending.

## How it works

- **One pill, start to finish.** The same element is the button, the field and the result, so you never lose your place. It resizes with Motion's `layout` on a slightly bouncy spring, and each change plays in beats: what leaves fades quickly, then the pill resizes, and what arrives comes in a moment later, the field first and then Send.
- **The person you invited joins the stack.** They're added to the front of the faces, so the others make room and their face arrives in the gap. It's picked out in green for a moment, then fades to match the rest.
- **The icon and the button's words change in place.** `Remold.Icon` reshapes the plus into the mail icon, and `Remold.Text` turns "Send" into "Sent" letter by letter.
- Errors are in the button and read out to screen readers. The field is marked invalid until you type again.
- The invite is pretend. Send the real one in `send`, and add the person once it succeeds.
