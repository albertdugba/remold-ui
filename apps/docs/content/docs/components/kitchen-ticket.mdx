It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Open the ticket.** It springs open from the status button, and a highlight glides to whatever you point at.
- **Cook the order.** The ring fills: empty when the ticket comes in, half on the grill, most of the way when it's at the pass.
- **Hand it over.** Picked up fills the circle and draws a check. Canceled draws a cross. Switch between the two and they reshape into each other.
- **Read the line.** Ticket in, on the grill, at the pass. For here and to-go use the same menu.

## How it works

- **One picker, used twice.** The ticket and for-here or to-go share a `Picker` built on Base UI's Select, with Motion on top for the opening spring and the gliding highlight.
- **Check and cross.** The two marks are reshaped into each other with Remold's `createMorph`; the ring's fill is a plain stroke animation.
