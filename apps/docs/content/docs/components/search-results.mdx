It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Open the field.** It rises from the middle to the top, and recent searches arrive under it. Click it again, press Escape or click anywhere else to close it.
- **Move through the list.** Use the arrow keys or the pointer. The highlight travels from row to row rather than switching on and off.
- **Choose one.** Press Enter or click. The word in the field turns into the one you chose: the letters both share slide into place and the rest fade. Then the menu closes and the field settles back.

## How it works

- One [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) wraps the field and the menu. Opening changes the field from centred to top-aligned and adds the menu; Layout moves the field and fades the menu in from that one change.
- The highlight is a box in the same grid cell as the active row. Moving it is a layout change, so the same Layout carries it from row to row.
- [Text](/docs/primitives/text) changes the word in the field.
- There are no CSS transitions in it. Everything that moves is Remold.
