## Things to try

- **Type a word and press comma.** The chip forms around the word without it moving.
- **Pick a suggestion.** It flies up into the field. A new word is offered as “Create”.
- **Press Backspace in an empty field.** The last tag comes off.

## How it works

- **Built on Base UI.** `Combobox` in `multiple` mode gives the chips, the suggestions, keyboard navigation and removal.
- **The text becomes the chip.** A new chip starts with no padding, no tint and no dot, exactly where the typed text sat, then all three grow in with Motion.
- **Every tag keeps its colour.** Known tags have one; a new tag gets one from its letters, so it's the same every time. The chip is a soft tint of it, and fills with it when you arrow back onto it to remove it.
- **Picked tags fly in.** Each suggestion is the tag's dot and word. Pick one and the word lifts out of its row and flies up into the field as a chip; its remove button grows in once it lands. Added tags leave the list, and come back when removed.
- **A limit you can see.** The counter under the field rolls as tags come and go, and turns amber at six, when the field stops taking more.
