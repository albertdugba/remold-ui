## Things to try

- **Drag a handle.** Its price moves from the label to above your finger.
- **Let go.** The price flies back into the label.
- **Watch the bars.** They darken inside your range, and the count below updates.

## How it works

- **Built on Base UI.** `Slider` with a thumb for each end gives dragging, keyboard and the minimum gap between them.
- **The price follows you.** The label's price and the bubble share a Motion `layoutId`, so it travels between them.

<Callout>The prices and counts are made up.</Callout>
