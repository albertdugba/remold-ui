It plays a short scene while it's on screen.

## Things to try

- **Go offline** with the switch. A banner slides in above the document saying your changes are kept on this device, and counts down to its next try.
- **Write something meanwhile.** It's marked as not yet synced.
- **Come back online.** The banner folds into a small green "Back online" pill, the marks clear, and the pill steps out of the way.
- **Turn off your network for real.** It listens to the browser's own online and offline events, not just the switch.

## How it works

- **Banner to pill.** The banner and the pill are different markup, so [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) animates the change, pairing them with no ids.
- The switch that simulates a dropped connection is Base UI's Switch.
