It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Type @.** A picker springs open right at the caret, not under the box.
- **Keep typing.** The list narrows to the people who still match, and the highlight glides between them as you point or use the arrow keys.
- **Pick someone.** Their face flies out of the list into the sentence as a chip, and they join the row of people who'll be notified.
- **Send.** The comment rises into the thread above.

## How it works

- **One face, a new place.** The avatar in the picker and the avatar in the chip are the same element moving, which is what Motion's `layoutId` is for, so the face travels from the list into the text instead of appearing there.
- The picker is Base UI's Popover, so focus, dismissal and positioning at the caret are handled for you; the Send button's shortcut hint is Base UI's Tooltip.
