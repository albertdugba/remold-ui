## Things to try

- **Enter an email and press Continue.** The field shrinks into a chip with your face on it, the title re-letters, and the password field arrives underneath.
- **Tap the chip.** It grows back into the email field so you can change it.
- **Leave the email half-typed.** The field shakes and says what's missing.
- **Type a short password.** Anything under eight characters is refused: the password field shakes and turns red.
- **Show the password.** The eye turns into a struck-through eye.
- **Sign in.** The button spins, becomes a check, and then your face travels up from the chip as the card greets you by name.
- **Use Google, GitHub or a passkey.** That button spins and takes you straight in.

Any email works, and any password of eight characters or more. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## How it works

- **The email becomes the chip.** The field's fill and the chip's fill share a Motion layout id, so the fill travels up and rounds into the chip instead of fading. Your face does the same, from the chip to the top of the card.
- **Steps arrive on a spring.** Each step's own pieces (the provider buttons, the password field) rise in on a Motion spring while the old step fades out, and `AnimatePresence` keeps the swap interruptible. The button stays put and only its label re-letters with Remold.
- **The card follows its content.** The content is measured and the card's height springs to it, so nothing jumps between steps.
- **Errors point at the field.** The message appears under the field that's wrong, which shakes once and takes a red outline. Typing clears it.
- **Focus follows the step.** Continue puts focus in the password field, the chip puts it back in the email field, and signing in moves it to Sign out.
