## Things to try

- **Press Add.** The button stretches into minus, count and plus.
- **Count up and down.** The number rolls like a counter.
- **Go back to one.** The minus becomes a bin; press it and the stepper folds back into Add.

## How it works

- **Built on Base UI.** `NumberField` gives increment, decrement, the arrow keys and the min and max.
- **The button becomes the stepper.** Both share a Motion `layoutId`. The minus reshapes into the bin with Remold's `createMorph`, and the count rolls with the Odometer.
