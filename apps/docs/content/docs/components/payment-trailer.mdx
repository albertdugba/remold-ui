## Things to try

- **Watch it play.** The phone floats with the amount on its screen, reshapes into a card that taps with contactless waves, becomes a bank while three coins pop out above it, the coins drop through the door, and the bank turns into a receipt with a green tick.
- **Pick a scene.** The bars under it jump straight to any scene, and every part reshapes from wherever it is.

## How it works

- **One drawing, Remold only.** The phone, the card, the bank and the receipt are the same five `Remold.PathGroup` parts, matched by id: the phone's screen becomes the card's chip, then the bank's window, then the receipt's paper. The eyes ride along on whatever they're on.
- **Travel by morphing.** The coins are their own parts that spring out of a dot above the bank and then into its door, so moving is just another reshape, with a little bounce.
- **Yours to rebrand.** Colours are CSS variables, so it follows dark mode and your brand, and the words are real text: pass `name` and `amount`.
- The float, the tap and the landing are plain CSS and stop for anyone who prefers reduced motion.
