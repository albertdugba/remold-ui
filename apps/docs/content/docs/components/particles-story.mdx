It moves to the next chapter every three seconds.

## Things to try

- **Watch the jacket.** The first chapter is a photo, sampled into a grid of dots by how dark each spot is.
- **See it flow.** The same dots travel into a music note, a parcel and an arrival check, then back to the jacket.
- **Move your pointer through it.** The dots push away from it and settle back.

## How it works

- **One field of dots.** [`Remold.Particles`](/docs/primitives/particles) keeps the same particles and gives them a new `shape` each chapter: an image sampled by darkness, then SVG paths. Each dot flies from where it is to its place in the next shape.
- **Sized to the stage.** The number of dots grows with the stage's width, and they're a little larger on the photo, so the picture stays readable on a phone.
- Pass `playing={false}` to hold it on the current chapter.

<Callout>The jacket photo is served from `/returns-field-jacket.jpg`. Put your own image in `public/` and change the path in the first chapter.</Callout>
