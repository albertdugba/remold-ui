## Things to try

- **Move your pointer over the card.** It tilts toward you in 3D and a soft sheen follows the pointer across it.
- **Tap the card, or Show details.** It flips over on a spring to its back: magnetic stripe, full number, expiry and CVC.
- **Copy a detail.** Each one has its own copy button, which reshapes into a green tick, and a line under the card says what was copied.
- **Wait.** A thin line along the bottom of the back shrinks over 15 seconds, then the card flips back to hide the details.

## How it works

- **Two faces, one turn.** The front and back are stacked with `backface-visibility: hidden`, the back pre-turned 180 degrees. Flipping springs the pair's `rotateY` from 0 to 180 inside a `perspective`, so it turns like a real card.
- **The tilt follows the pointer.** Pointer position feeds Motion springs for `rotateX`, `rotateY` and the sheen's position, so the card leans and settles smoothly and stops when the pointer leaves. It only tilts for a mouse, and not with reduced motion.
- **Icons reshape.** The eye and copy icons are `Remold.Icon`, and the button label re-letters with `Remold.Text`.
