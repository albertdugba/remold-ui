It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Add a label.** "Add label" opens a picker with a search box, and a highlight glides to what you point at.
- **Pick one.** It drops onto the issue as a chip while the others make room.
- **Type a new name.** The list offers to create it, with a colour that pops in.
- **Take a chip off** with its ×.

## How it works

- The picker is Base UI's Popover, so focus moves into the search box and back to the button when it closes.
- The gliding highlight and the chips making room are Motion.
