## Things to try

- **Click the search pill, or press /.** The pill springs wider and taller into a card, with the field still at its top; then your recent searches and what's popular right now rise in inside it, and the page behind dims a little.
- **Pick a recent search** to fill the field, or **type "chi"**. The list narrows to the chicken dishes, each with its photo and restaurant; what you typed is picked out, and the rows that stay slide into place.
- **Press a +.** It fills in as the dish goes in your basket, and the total on the basket button rolls.
- **Use the arrow keys.** One highlight slides from row to row, and Enter picks it.
- **Press Escape, or click outside.** The results fade first, then the card folds back into the pill.

## How it works

- **One shape.** The pill and the card are the same element: it springs from where the pill rests to a centred card sized to its results, and its fill crossfades from the pill's grey to the card's white as it grows. Its height follows the results, so typing makes it shrink or grow to fit.
- **Contents follow the shape.** It's Base UI's Autocomplete, with the popup rendered inside the card. Its contents wait a beat on the way in so they arrive once there's room, and leave first on the way out, so the card never shrinks over them.
- **A calm backdrop.** The page dims slightly while you search; clicking it closes the search.
- **Built for the keyboard.** The field is a combobox: "/" focuses it from anywhere on the page, the first match is highlighted as you type so Enter picks it, Escape closes, and the footer says so. Each add button says what it adds.
