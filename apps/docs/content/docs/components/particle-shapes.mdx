## Things to try

- **Watch it play.** The galaxy turns, then its arms unwind and stretch into two twisting strands. The strands loop into the Lorenz attractor's two wings, which fold into a honeybee, stripes, wings and all, then open out into a butterfly and close into the Grok mark before it gathers back into the galaxy.
- **Move your cursor through it.** The dots part around the pointer and drift back as it leaves, as if you were moving through space.
- **Pick a shape.** It stops playing and flows straight there, from wherever the dots are.
- **Customize it.** Change how many dots there are and how they look, how the camera turns and leans, and how one shape becomes the next: a scatter or a direct spring, how far the cloud spreads and how long it holds, and how the dots pair up. The code below the controls updates to match.

## How it works

- **Shapes from parts.** Each shape is built with `shapes` from `@remold-ui/core`: `spiral`, `helix` and `attractor("lorenz")` are ready-made, the bee and the butterfly are each a `compose` of ellipsoids, spheres, curves and ovals, and the mark is its SVG path made solid with `fromPath`. The parts are moved with `.at`, `.rotate` and `.mirror` and coloured with `.tone` and `.stripes`.
- **Remold draws it.** `<Remold.Particles>` takes the shape and a `count`, turns it with `camera`, and makes overlapping dots glow with `blend="add"`. Colours come from a named `palette`, so each part says `"gold"` or `"wing"` rather than a number.
- **A scatter in between.** `transition="scatter"` dissolves each shape into a glittering cloud that spreads through the depth too, holds a moment, then gathers into the next.
- **One flow, not a swarm.** `pairing="flow"` pairs dots by where they sit along each shape, so the galaxy's core becomes the top of the helix and the path's start becomes the bee's tail.
- **Cheap when it can be.** It only draws while on screen, stops turning when paused (`camera={{ spin: 0 }}`), and uses fewer dots on narrow screens. Behind it, a `<Remold.Canvas>` paints a still field of faint stars once.
