## Things to try

- **Create a key.** The card springs into a name form, and "Create key" travels down to become its submit button.
- **Reveal the secret.** Create turns the card into a dark panel where the secret decodes a character at a time.
- **Copy it.** The copy icon turns into a check.
- **Press Done.** The card springs back to the list, the secret slides into the newest row, and scrambles itself into its masked form.
- **Revoke a key.** Hover a row (or tab to it) and press Revoke. It turns into a red Confirm; press it and the row folds out of the list while the header says it's gone. Escape or tabbing away takes it back.

## How it works

- **One card, three faces.** The list, the name form and the secret are faces of one card. It springs its real height between them and clips what's inside, so nothing is scaled; each face's parts blur and scale in from just below their size.
- **Things that travel.** "Create key" and the secret move with Motion's `layoutId`: the button into the form, the secret from the panel into its row.
- **Decode and mask.** Revealing and hiding the secret use the same scramble hook, run in opposite directions. The subtitle reshapes with `Remold.Text` to say what each step wants.
- **Revoking asks in place.** The first press only arms it: Revoke turns into a red Confirm. Once it's gone the row folds away by height, and the card follows it exactly.
- **Built on Base UI.** The Live and Test switch is Base UI's Toggle Group.
