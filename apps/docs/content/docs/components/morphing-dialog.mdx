## Things to try

- **Press New habit.** The button's dark surface grows into the dialog while everything behind it dims, turning white as it arrives. Then the form fades in.
- **Cancel, or press Escape.** The dialog shrinks back into the button it came from.
- **Name a habit and press Add habit.** The button shows a spinner, then the dialog shrinks into the new habit at the top of the list, marked New. The other habits make room.
- **Pick how often.** The selection slides between Daily, Weekdays, Weekly and Monthly.

## How it works

- **One surface, three places.** The button's background, the dialog's background and the new row's background share a layout id, so it's one surface moving between them. It's an empty shape, so growing it never stretches any words; the form fades in once it has arrived, and its colour and corners change on the way.
- **The dialog becomes what it made.** On create, the surface is handed to the new row instead of back to the button, and the button gets a fresh one. Pass `surface` and bump it each time something is created.
- **A real modal.** It's Base UI's Dialog: focus moves to the habit field and stays inside, Escape and Cancel close it, focus returns to the button, and the title and description are announced. The habit is a labelled Field and how often a Toggle Group.
- **It waits for the request.** While creating, the form locks and the button shows a spinner at the same width, so nothing shifts. In the demo a timeout stands in for the request.
- **Mount it where you like.** By default it mounts on the page and dims all of it. The demo passes its own stage as `container`, so the dim covers just the frame it sits in, and it turns `modal` off until you touch it, so it can play on its own without taking over the page.
