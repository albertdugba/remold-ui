## Things to try

- **Keep the streak.** The grey ember reshapes into a full flame and catches colour.
- **Watch the burst.** Sparks fly out of the flame as it lands, and the day count rolls up by one.
- **Look at the week.** Today's dot fills in alongside the days you've already kept.

## How it works

- **The ember grows into the flame.** The flame is one path reshaped with Remold's `createMorph`, so the ember genuinely becomes the flame rather than one icon swapping for another.
- The sparks, the colour and the rolling count are Motion.
