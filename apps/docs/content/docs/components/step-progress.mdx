## Things to try

- **Press Continue.** The dot in the current marker turns into a check, the line to the next step turns green, and the next marker gets its dot.
- **Press Back,** or tap a step you've finished. The check turns back into a dot, and the line goes grey again.
- **Reach Review.** The button says Place order. Press it and every step is checked, and the caption says All done.

## How it works

- Each marker is an [Icon](/docs/primitives/icon) that changes between three icons: an empty circle, a circle with a dot, and a check. Icon morphs from one to the next, so the dot turns into the check rather than swapping.
- The lines are [Connectors](/docs/primitives/connectors): each marker is an anchor linked to the one before it, so the lines stay attached however the row is laid out. A step that's done marks the line after it `line="done"`, and that line turns green.
- The caption and the buttons are [Text](/docs/primitives/text), so "Step 2 of 4" and "Continue" change letter by letter instead of swapping all at once.
- It's an ordered list, and the current step carries `aria-current="step"`. Steps ahead of you are disabled; steps you've done take you back.
