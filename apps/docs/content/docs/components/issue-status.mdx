It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Open the status menu.** It springs open from the status button, and a highlight glides to whatever you point at.
- **Move the work along.** The icon fills its ring: empty for todo, half for in progress, three quarters in review.
- **Close it out.** Done and canceled fill the circle and draw a check or a cross. Switch between the two and they reshape into each other.
- **Read the activity.** Each change lands in the activity below. Priority uses the same menu.

## How it works

- **One picker, used twice.** Status and priority share a `Picker` built on Base UI's Select, with Motion on top for the opening spring and the gliding highlight.
- **Check and cross.** The two marks are reshaped into each other with Remold's `createMorph`; the ring's fill is a plain stroke animation.
