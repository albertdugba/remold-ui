## Things to try

- **Change the name, username or bio, or flip Public profile.** The save bar springs up from below and counts your edits: "1 unsaved change", "2 unsaved changes".
- **Change something back.** The count goes down, and if nothing is different any more the bar drops away by itself.
- **Press Save.** The button spins, then the bar's contents fade, the pill shrinks to fit, and a green tick and Saved fade in before it leaves.
- **Press Discard.** Every field goes back to what was saved and the bar drops.

## How it works

- **Saved and draft live side by side.** The form keeps what was last saved next to what you're editing. The bar shows whenever they differ, and the count is the number of fields that differ, so undoing a change by hand counts down.
- **Base UI does the fields.** Name, username and bio are each a `Field`, and the bio is a `Field.Control` rendered as a `textarea`. Public profile is a `Switch`, and its caption re-letters to say who can see the profile.
- **One pill, reshaped by Remold.** The bar's contents sit inside a `Remold.Morph`. On save the old contents fade first, then the pill springs from its old box to its new one, so nothing inside it stretches and nothing is left floating outside it.
- **Motion brings it in and out.** The bar enters and leaves on a spring with a short blur.
- **The count re-letters.** The count uses `Remold.Text`, so only the characters that change move.
