It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Switch between 7, 30 and 90 days.** The chart line doesn't redraw: it reshapes into the new range.
- **Watch the numbers.** The MRR rolls like a counter, and the change badge rolls too, its arrow turning between up and down.
- **Scrub along the chart.** A dot rides the line and the headline shows that day's figure.

## How it works

- **One line, reshaped.** The chart line and the badge's arrow are paths morphed with Remold's [`createMorph`](/docs/core/shapes), so a new range is the same line moving into new points, and up and down are the same arrow turning.
- The range switch is Base UI's Toggle Group; the figures roll with a small odometer built on Motion.
