Move your pointer over it, or click to send a ripple across.

## Things to try

- **Move around.** The dots under the pointer swell, brighten and lean away, and fade back as you leave.
- **Click anywhere.** A ripple runs out across the grid from where you clicked.
- **Press Get started.** The ripple starts from the button, whether you click it or press it with the keyboard.

## How it works

- **The background is one component.** [Remold.DotGrid](/docs/primitives/dot-grid) sits behind the content with `pointer-events: none` and follows the pointer over its parent, so the headline and buttons stay clickable.
- **Colours are CSS.** The canvas's `color` paints the resting dots and `accent-color` the lit ones, so dark mode is two variables. A radial `mask-image` fades the grid out at the edges.
- **The demo cursor uses the same controls.** The scene calls `hover` and `ripple` through `actionsRef`, the same way a product tour could.
- **It's quiet when nothing happens.** It only draws while dots are moving, and pauses off screen.

<Callout>Swap the headline and buttons for your own. To light up your wordmark in the dots, pass `shape={{ text: "yourname" }}`.</Callout>
