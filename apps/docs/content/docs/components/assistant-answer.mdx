It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Ask a question.** The orb hollows into a turning ring while it thinks.
- **Read the answer.** The ring scatters and gathers into the answer's key figure, with the sentence underneath.
- **Poke the orb.** The dots lean away from your pointer.

## How it works

- **One cloud, three shapes.** The orb and ring are point lists, and the headline is a plain `{ text, font }` shape. The primitive samples and fits it.
- **Thinking is a new ring every tick.** Each tick turns the ring a little, and the dots' springs blend the steps into a spin.
- **The answer scatters in.** `transition="scatter"` breaks the ring apart and gathers it into the headline.

<Callout>The answers are canned. Pull the key figure out of your model's reply and use it as the headline.</Callout>
