It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Hold it.** The mic morphs into a red record dot, "Hold to record" rewrites itself into the time, and the waveform grows out of the pill as you speak.
- **Let go.** The dot morphs into play and the waveform settles into the finished clip.
- **Press ×.** The clip folds back: the bars tuck away, the time rewrites itself into "Hold to record", and play morphs back into the mic.
- **Just tap.** A tap is too short to record, so it asks you to keep holding.
- **Use the keyboard.** Focus the pill, hold Space or Enter, and let go to keep it. Escape cancels.

## How it works

- **Nothing is swapped.** The pill has the same four parts in every phase: the icon, the words, the waveform and the ×. Phases only change what they show and how wide they are, so each part reshapes instead of fading out while a new one fades in.
- Motion lays out the pill and controls across phases; the waveform and clock are ordinary updates.
- **Holding** uses pointer capture, so the gesture survives a finger drifting off the pill.

The levels are generated so the demo needs no microphone. In an app, read them from an `AnalyserNode` on the `MediaRecorder`'s stream; nothing else changes.
