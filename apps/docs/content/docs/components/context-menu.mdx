## Things to try

- **Click a folder** to open or close it. The chevron turns and the rows slide.
- **Right-click a campaign or folder.** On a phone, press and hold it. The menu springs out from where you pressed, and the row you picked lights up.
- **Go three levels deep.** New ▸ From template ▸ Retargeting ▸ Cart abandoners. The new campaign slides into the folder, opening it if it was closed.
- **Change its status.** Status ▸ Active. The dot turns green and Paused re-letters into its daily budget.
- **Move it.** Move to lists folders, and folders with folders inside open their own submenu, so you can walk Q4 Holiday ▸ Black Friday.
- **Copy, duplicate or archive.** Copy link shows its address in the pill under the workspace. Duplicate slides a draft copy in beside it. Archiving a folder takes everything inside it, and Undo brings it all back.

## How it works

- **Base UI does the menu.** `ContextMenu` opens on right-click and long-press, positions the menu at the pointer, and handles the keyboard, focus and dismissal. `SubmenuRoot` nests menus three deep under New and as deep as your folders go under Move to. Status is a `RadioGroup`, so it reads as one choice.
- **A few small pieces build every menu.** `Panel` is the glass with its own sliding highlight, `Submenu` is a row that opens another panel, and `Item` is an icon, a label and shortcut keys. The highlight comes from context, so nested panels each get their own.
- **It opens from where you pressed.** Base UI puts the pointer's position in `--transform-origin`, and the menu scales up from that point on a springy Motion entrance. Each submenu grows out of the row that opened it.
- **Rows slide in and out.** New, moved, duplicated and archived rows animate their height, so the rows around them move instead of jumping. Budgets and the status pill re-letter with `Remold.Text`.
- **One value.** `value` holds the entries, which folders are open, whether the menu is open and on which row, and the last change for Undo. `addEntry`, `setStatus`, `moveEntry`, `duplicateEntry` and `archiveEntry` are plain functions, so the same changes can be scripted.
