## Things to try

- **Right-click a page.** On a phone, press and hold it. The menu springs out from where you pressed, and the page you picked turns blue.
- **Go three levels deep.** Add ▸ From template ▸ Meetings ▸ Weekly sync. The new page slides in under the one you clicked, opening it if it was closed.
- **Change its icon.** Icon shows an emoji grid. Pick one and the page's emoji spins into the new one.
- **Move it.** Move to lists the other top-level pages. The page slides out of one place and into the other.
- **Copy, duplicate or delete.** Copy link puts its address in the pill under the sidebar. Duplicate slides a copy in beside it. Deleting a page takes its sub-pages too, and Undo brings them all back.
- **Click a page with sub-pages** to open or close it. The chevron turns and the rows slide.

## How it works

- **Base UI does the menu.** `ContextMenu` opens on right-click and long-press, positions the menu at the pointer, and handles the keyboard, focus and dismissal. `SubmenuRoot` nests menus, three deep under Add. The emoji grid is a `RadioGroup`, so it reads as one choice.
- **A few small pieces build every menu.** `Panel` is the glass with its own sliding highlight, `Submenu` is a row that opens another panel, and `Item` is an icon or emoji, a label and shortcut keys. The highlight comes from context, so nested panels each get their own.
- **It opens from where you pressed.** Base UI puts the pointer's position in `--transform-origin`, and the menu scales up from that point on a springy Motion entrance. Each submenu grows out of the row that opened it.
- **Rows slide in and out.** New, moved, duplicated and deleted pages animate their height, so the rows around them move instead of jumping. The status pill re-letters with `Remold.Text` to say what changed.
