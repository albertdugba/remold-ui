It steps through the layers by itself while it's on screen. Hover or tap a label and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Hover a label.** That layer's sides fill with its colour and a little sheen, every layer above it fades to a ghost outline so you can see down to it, and its label turns dark with a short caption.
- **Move between labels quickly.** Colours, ghosts and labels change on springs, so they turn round mid-way instead of jumping.
- **Drag the stack sideways** to turn it. The labels are real HTML, and they follow their layers every frame.

`layers` takes your own list, top to bottom: `{ id, name, role, color, icon }`, where `icon` is a 24 × 24 outline icon as path data.

## How it works

- Each layer is `extrude({ ink: true, sides: "sheen" })` on the `sketch` palette. There's one scene layout per layer, and in each one the focused layer has `fill: 1` and its `color`, and the layers above it have `ghost`. All three spring between layouts, like position and size.
- `ghost` fades a solid's fills but keeps its ink edges, and shows its far edges through it: a see-through outline rather than a faded one.
- The labels are buttons laid over the canvas. After every frame, the scene's `onDraw` places each one level with its layer's wall (`anchor(id, "front" | "right")`), and the leader line reaches exactly to the edge.
