## Things to try

- **Pick the kind of visit.** A check-up, a follow-up or a consultation. The option fills in dark, then that same dark shape flies up into the first slot of the summary and becomes a chip, and the next step slides in.
- **Pick a day, then a time.** Full days and taken times can't be chosen. Each choice follows the first into the summary.
- **Tap a chip.** You're back on that step, and the chip flies back down onto the option it came from.
- **Confirm the booking.** After a short spinner, the steps give way to the confirmation, with the end time worked out from the kind of visit and a reminder promised by text.

## How it works

- **The choice is one shape that moves.** A chosen option sits on a dark surface that keeps one layout id from the option to its chip. It's an empty shape, so growing or shrinking it never stretches words; the chip's words fade in where it lands.
- **The steps take turns.** Each step cross-fades sideways into the next, and the panel springs its height to fit, so the card changes size without jumping.
- **Built on Base UI.** Each step is a Radio Group, so arrow keys move between options and unavailable ones are disabled. The chips are buttons that say what they hold, and the confirmation is announced.
