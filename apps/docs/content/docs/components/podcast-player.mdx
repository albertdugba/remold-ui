Tap an episode to open its player; the arrow takes you back to the list.

## Things to try

- **Open an episode.** Its tile grows to fill the panel: the artwork becomes the cover, the title lifts into place beside it with a short description of the episode under it, and the other episodes fade away. The blurred backdrop takes on that episode's colours.
- **Press play.** The sound waves start to move, and the part already played darkens. The player has a scrubber, skip back 15 and forward 30, and on this page it plays the real episode.
- **Go back** with the arrow. The cover shrinks back into exactly the tile it came from, and the other episodes return around it.
- **Open another** straight away. Every change starts from where things are on screen, so it never has to finish one move before starting the next.

## How it works

- **One list, two shapes.** The grid and the player are the same list. Open an episode and the list holds just that one, laid out as a player; [Morph](/docs/primitives/morph) works out that its tile is still the same element and moves and resizes it, so the cover never fades or jumps.
- **The backdrop follows the episode.** The artwork is drawn again, blurred and saturated, behind everything, and fades to the next one when you open a different episode.
- Play and pause are one [Icon](/docs/primitives/icon) that morphs between the two symbols.
- `episodes` takes `{ id, show, title, artwork, colors, audio, seconds }`. With `audio` the player plays the file; without it, play just runs the clock. When an episode ends, the next one starts.
