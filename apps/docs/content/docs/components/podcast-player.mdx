Tap any episode to open its details; the back arrow returns to the grid.

## Things to try

- **Open an episode.** The small tile's artwork grows into the big hero, and the title — which sat on top of the artwork in the grid — lifts off and settles next to it. The two views share no ids; the engine matches the episode and its title by what they show.
- **Press play.** The details view has an inline player — scrubber, skip back 15, forward 30, speed — and on this page it plays the real episode.
- **Go back.** The hero shrinks back into exactly the tile it came from, in reverse.
- **Open another halfway through.** Every change starts from where things are on screen, so switching mid-flight just turns toward the new episode.

## How it works

- The grid and the details page are two different pieces of markup. One [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) wraps them with `trigger={[view, currentId]}` and matches what they share — the artwork and the title — by content, then springs each from its old box to its new one. No `layoutId`s to wire up.
- The artwork is the same image in both places, so it flies from the tile to the hero and resizes as it goes; the title moves from its overlay on the artwork to its spot beside it, changing colour on the way. The play/pause button is one [Icon](/docs/primitives/icon) that morphs between the two symbols.
- `episodes` takes `{ id, show, title, artwork, colors, audio, seconds, date, summary }`. With `audio` the player plays the file; without it, play just runs the clock.
