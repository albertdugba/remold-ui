Press play, or tap the bar, to open the player; the chevron folds it back.

## Things to try

- **Open the player.** The thumbnail grows into the cover, the title and show lift into place under it, and the play button settles into the middle of the controls.
- **Press play.** The player has a scrubber, skip back 15 and forward 30, and on this page it plays the real episode.
- **Fold it back** with the chevron. The cover shrinks back into exactly the thumbnail it came from.
- **Open it halfway through closing.** Every change starts from where things are on screen, so it just turns around.

## How it works

- The mini bar and full player are different markup. A Motion `LayoutGroup` gives the artwork, title, show and play control matching `layoutId`s, so the compact player genuinely expands rather than being swapped.
- The play button is the same dark disc in both shapes, only bigger, so it reads as one control. Play and pause are one [Icon](/docs/primitives/icon) that morphs between the two symbols.
- `episodes` takes `{ id, show, title, artwork, colors, audio, seconds }`, and the first one plays. With `audio` the player plays the file; without it, play just runs the clock.
