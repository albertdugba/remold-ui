It walks through the categories by itself while it's on screen. Pick a tab or tap a tile and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Pick a category.** The integrations in it step forward into a ring around Lumen and their lines light up with flowing data. The rest step back to the outer ring and sink.
- **Watch Webhooks and Keyring.** They belong to two categories, so between Data and Messaging (or Data and Payments) they stay lit and only move.
- **Tap a tile.** It lifts, everything else dims with its line, and the line underneath says what it does. Tap it again, or the floor, to let go.
- **Drag sideways** to turn the scene. It keeps turning for a moment when you let go.

## Make it yours

- **Your product** is the `hub` object. Swap the `SUN` icon for your mark (any 24 × 24 outline path).
- **Your integrations** are the `INTEGRATIONS` list: a name, an outline icon, the categories it belongs to and one line about it. Categories become the tabs.
- **The look** comes from canvas `extrude()`: corner radius, height, and the icon on top. The light colours are the `paper` palette.

## How it works

- Every category is a layout of the same objects. [Scene](/docs/primitives/scene) springs each tile from where it is to where it is in the next layout, so a tile in both categories glides instead of disappearing and coming back.
- Each line belongs to the layouts its tile is a member of, so lines fade in and out with their tiles. Dots run along them while they're shown.
- Tapping focuses a tile: it lifts and everything else dims to 30%, lines included, which is what makes its path stand out.
