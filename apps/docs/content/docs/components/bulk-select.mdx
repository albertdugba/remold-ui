It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Tick a row, then shift-click another.** Everything between is selected, and neighbouring picks join into one band.
- **Watch the header box.** It shows when some or all of the rows are selected.
- **Look at the foot.** The hint gives way to an action bar whose count rolls as the selection grows.
- **Delete.** The chosen rows fold away one after another, and the bar offers Undo.

## How it works

- The checkboxes, including the header's mixed state, are Base UI's Checkbox; the action bar's buttons have Base UI tooltips.
- The joined bands, the rolling count and the rows folding away are Motion.
