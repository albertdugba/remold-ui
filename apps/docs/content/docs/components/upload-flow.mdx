It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Drop an image on it, or click it.** Your picture fills the frame and uploads. The upload is pretend and nothing leaves your browser.
- **Cancel halfway.** The frame empties again.
- **Copy the link.** The button says it's done.
- **Choose another.** Click the picture to replace it.

## How it works

- One [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) wraps the card and runs on every step. The progress bar's fill is a width, so Layout grows it. The picture, the check and each new line of the caption fade in, and whatever leaves fades out.
- [Text](/docs/primitives/text) rolls the byte count, the one thing that changes by a few digits rather than becoming a different sentence.
- The frame never changes size, so nothing around it moves while it uploads.
- Wire `start` to your real upload and drive `sent` from its progress events.
