It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Open Filter.** A menu of fields opens, each with its values in a submenu. Picking one adds a segmented token to the bar.
- **Change a token's value.** Click it and the new value rolls into place.
- **Remove a token** with its ×. The bar closes the gap.
- **Watch the list.** The transactions slide into their new places, and both the count and the total spent roll to their new figures.

## How it works

- The menu and its submenus are Base UI's Menu, so keyboard navigation between fields and values works out of the box.
- The tokens, the reordering rows and the rolling figures are Motion.

<Callout>The spending is made up. The merchants' marks are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org).</Callout>
