It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Open the room.** The three faces stacked in the pill fan out onto the stage, each to its own spot, and the listeners fill in below.
- **Raise your hand.** A small hand appears on your face among the listeners. When the host invites you a moment later, your face moves up onto the stage.
- **Watch who's talking.** A soft ring pulses around whoever is speaking. Muted speakers are skipped.
- **Leave the stage**, or minimise the room back into the pill.

## How it works

- **Same faces, different markup.** The pill's stack and the room's stage are separate pieces of markup with nothing tying them together. [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) pairs each face by the image it shows, so a face in the stack becomes the same face on the stage, and your face in the listener grid becomes yours on stage.
- **Only two things move.** Layout's `trigger` is whether the room is open and whether you're on stage. Whose turn it is to speak is plain CSS, so the ring never moves anything else.
- The pill and the room match too, by the title and the listener count they both show, so the pill grows into the room rather than being swapped for it.

<Callout>The people and the room are made up. Avatars are illustrations from [DiceBear](https://www.dicebear.com) (the Notionists style).</Callout>
