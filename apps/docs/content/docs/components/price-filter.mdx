## Things to try

- **Step it.** Press − or + to move the max price by $10, or hold either to keep going. The price rolls, the histogram's bars light up to the new price, and the count of lamps under it rolls too.
- **Drag the price.** Press the price and drag it sideways: the stepper stretches into a slider from $0 to $500, and the price becomes the thumb under your pointer. Let go and it folds back into the stepper.
- **Type it.** Click the price without dragging and type one; arrow keys step it too.

## How it works

- **One control, two shapes.** The pill springs its real width between the stepper and the slider and clips what's inside, so nothing is stretched. Exact steps when you know the number; a slider when you want to sweep.
- **One value, always there.** The price is a single pill laid over both shapes. It springs from the middle of the stepper to the slider's thumb when the shape changes, and follows the thumb closely while you drag.
- **No jump when it opens.** The drag is measured from where you pressed, so the slider starts at the price you already had rather than wherever your pointer lands on the track.
- **The result is the point.** The histogram shows how prices are spread across the category, and the count says what the filter leaves you with, so you can see a good cut-off before you let go.
- **Built on Base UI.** `NumberField` gives the stepper its typing, arrow keys and press-and-hold; `Slider` gives the range its keyboard and accessible value. The price and the count roll with the Odometer.

<Callout>The prices are made up. Pass in your own, and filter your products by the value.</Callout>
