It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Watch the count.** The number of people already waiting ticks up as you watch.
- **Join.** The form isn't swapped for a message: the button slides across and becomes the confirmation's check, and the field reshapes into the panel around it. The perks underneath fold away.
- **Use a different email** to play it backwards.

## How it works

- **Only plain shapes are shared.** The field's background and the button's fill are the two things that move between the states, so nothing with text in it ever stretches. The words cross-fade on top.
- The field is Base UI's Field and Input; the movement is Motion, and the count rolls with a small odometer.
