## Things to try

- **Add someone.** Tap a face under "Also at the table": it flies up into the split, the bar re-divides and every share rolls down.
- **Leave someone out.** Press × on their row and they go back to the table; the others' shares grow.
- **Change the tip.** The total and every share roll to the new amount.
- **Request the money.** The button asks the others for what they owe, then says it's sent.
- **Look at the cents.** The shares always add up to the bill exactly.

## How it works

- **One face, two places.** Each person's face is the same Motion element in the tray and in the list, so it travels between them when they join or leave the split.
- **The bill re-divides.** Each slice of the bar is a Motion flex item, so they resize together; the amounts roll with the Odometer.
- **Built on Base UI.** `Button` for adding, removing and requesting; `ToggleGroup` for the tip. `SplitAmount` takes `value` / `onValueChange` (who's paying and the tip) or runs on its own, plus your `diners` and an `onRequest` hook.
- **No lost cents.** What's left after an even split goes a cent at a time to the first people.
