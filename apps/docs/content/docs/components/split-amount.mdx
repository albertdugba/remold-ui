## Things to try

- **Add someone.** The bar re-divides and every share rolls down.
- **Take someone out.** Their slice closes and the others grow.
- **Look at the cents.** The shares always add up to the bill exactly.

## How it works

- **Built on Base UI.** `ToggleGroup` with `multiple` is the row of people.
- **The bill re-divides.** Each slice is a Motion flex item, so they resize together; shares roll with the Odometer.
- **No lost cents.** What's left after an even split goes a cent at a time to the first people.
