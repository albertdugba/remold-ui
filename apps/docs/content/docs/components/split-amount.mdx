## Things to try

- **Add someone.** Tap a faded face. It lights up, its plus turns into a tick, the bar gains a slice and your share rolls down.
- **Leave someone out.** Tap them again and your share grows back.
- **Change the tip.** The total and your share roll to the new amount.
- **Request the money.** The button asks the others for what they owe, then says it's sent.
- **Look at the cents.** The shares always add up to the bill exactly.

## How it works

- **The bill re-divides.** Each slice of the bar is a Motion flex item, so they resize together. The dark slice is yours; the rest is what you get back.
- **The amounts roll.** Your share, the total and the figure on the button use the Odometer, so only the digits that change move.
- **The plus becomes a tick.** Each badge is a `Remold.Icon`, so its strokes reshape instead of swapping.
- **Built on Base UI.** The faces are a `ToggleGroup` that allows several at once, so they work from the keyboard and say whether they're pressed; the tip is a second one. `SplitAmount` takes `value` / `onValueChange` (who's paying and the tip) or runs on its own, plus your `diners` and an `onRequest` hook.
- **No lost cents.** What's left after an even split goes a cent at a time to the first people.
