It plays a month while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Watch the month go by.** The bars fill, turn amber when you're running low and red once you're over.
- **Look at the bill.** The overage slides into the projected bill.
- **Upgrade.** The new limits make every bar spring back down while the warnings fold away.
- **Point at the ⓘ beside a meter** to see what it counts and what going over costs.

## How it works

- Each bar is Base UI's Meter, so it carries the right role and values for assistive tech; the details are Base UI's Tooltip.
- The fills, colours and folding warnings are Motion, and the numbers roll with a small odometer.
