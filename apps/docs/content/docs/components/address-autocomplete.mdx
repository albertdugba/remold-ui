## Things to try

- **Type “12 Ox”.** Matching addresses are offered.
- **Pick one.** It rests in the box for a beat, then the area and city glide down out of the line while the new fields unfold from behind the street, one after the other.
- **Search again.** The fields fold back into the search.

## How it works

- **Built on Base UI.** `Autocomplete` does the suggestions and keyboard handling; `Field` gives each part its label.
- **One line becomes three.** The search box becomes the street field, and each part of the address keeps a Motion `layoutId` from the line to its field, so the words themselves travel. The fields show their own text once the words land.
- **No jumps.** The element reserves the split form's height from the start, so splitting grows downwards instead of moving what you were looking at.
