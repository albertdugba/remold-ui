It plays a short scene while it's on screen. Touch it and it's yours.

## Things to try

- **Switch to Yearly.** The thumb slides across, $24 rolls to $19, the billing line changes and "Save 20%" comes in beside the price.
- **Switch back halfway.** Every change starts from where things are on screen, so it just turns around.

## How it works

- The switch is a two-column grid, and the thumb sits in the chosen option's cell. Choosing moves it to the other cell, and [Layout](/docs/primitives/layout) carries it there.
- [Text](/docs/primitives/text) changes the price, so only the digits that differ move.
- The badge is at the end of the price line, so its arrival shifts nothing. Layout fades it in.
- Replace `PLAN` with your own prices and notes.
